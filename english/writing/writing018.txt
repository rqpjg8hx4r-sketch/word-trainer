#title: Writing 018 | Email: A cinema trip

#question

You would like to go to the cinema with your English friend, Andi.
Write an email to Andi.

In your email:
- ask Andi to go to the cinema with you
- say why you want to go to the cinema
- explain how you will travel there.

Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Andi,

Would you like to go to the cinema with me on Saturday? I want to watch Spider-Man because I love superhero films. We can go there by bus. I am sure it will be fun.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

邀请 → 去电影院的原因 → 交通方式 → 友好结尾

Invite: Would you like to go to the cinema with me on Saturday?

Reason: I want to watch Spider-Man because I love superhero films.

Travel: We can go there by bus.

Friendly ending: I am sure it will be fun.

记住这个邮件结构：Invite → Reason → Travel → Friendly ending

三个 task points：
邀请 Andi 一起去电影院 → Would you like to go to the cinema with me?
为什么想去 → I want to watch Spider-Man because I love superhero films.
怎么去 → by bus

这篇正文共 35 词，包含称呼和署名共 39 词。
题目没有指定日期，本篇把活动安排在 Saturday，让邀请更具体。
继续使用已熟悉的 Spider-Man 作为个人电影素材。
电影名称要配上“想看这部电影”的表达，明确回答为什么想去。
最后一个任务点是交通方式，不要遗漏，也不要只写到达时间。

实用句块：
Would you like to go to … with me?
I want to watch … because …
We can go there by …
I am sure it will be fun.

superhero films = 超级英雄电影
by bus = 乘公交车
by bike = 骑自行车
by car = 乘汽车
on foot = 步行

want to 后用动词原形：watch。
can 后用动词原形：go。
there 前不加 to：go there，不写 go to there。
by bus 中 bus 前不加 a；步行用 on foot。

和 writing001 对比：
001：Invite → Suggest a film → Explain why the friend will enjoy it
018：Invite → Explain why you want to go → Travel
两题都邀请看电影，但任务点不同，不能直接照搬整篇。
