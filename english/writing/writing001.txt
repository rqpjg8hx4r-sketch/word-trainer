#title: Writing 001 | Email: A cinema invitation

#question

You would like to go to the cinema on Saturday. Write an email to your
English friend, Jay.

In your email: - invite Jay to come with you - suggest a film to see -
explain why Jay will enjoy the film.

Write 25 words or more.

Write the email on your answer sheet.

#answer

Hi Jay,

Would you like to go to the cinema with me on Saturday? We can watch
Spider-Man. I think you will enjoy it because it is exciting and funny.

See you soon!
Alex

#tip

邀请 → 建议 → because 给理由 → 结束语

Invite: Would you like to … with me?

Suggest: We can …

Give a reason: I think you will enjoy it because …

End: See you soon!

记住这个写作结构： Invite → Suggest → Reason → End

这个结构也可以用于 cinema、party、sports、picnic 等类似的邮件题。
