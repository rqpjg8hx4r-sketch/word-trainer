#title: Writing 013 | Story: A letter to Grandma

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Anna wrote a letter to her grandma and put a photo in the
envelope. Then she went to the postbox and posted it. A few days
later, her grandma received the letter. She was very happy to read
it and see the photo.

#tip

写信并放入照片 → 寄信 → 收到信后的感受

Preparation: Anna wrote a letter to her grandma and put a photo in the envelope.

Action: Then she went to the postbox and posted it.

Result: A few days later, her grandma received the letter.

Feeling: She was very happy to read it and see the photo.

记住这个故事结构：Preparation → Action → Result → Feeling

实用句块：
One day, … wrote a letter to …
… put a photo in the envelope.
Then … went to the postbox and posted it.
A few days later, … received the letter.
… was very happy to …

这篇答案共 45 词，三幅图的主要内容都要写到：
女孩写信，旁边有照片和信封 → 女孩把信投入邮筒 → 老人读信、看照片。
把女孩命名为 Anna，把收信人设定为她的奶奶，让人物关系简单清楚。
把照片写进故事，可以连接第一幅和第三幅图。

用 One day 开始故事，Then 连接下一步行动，A few days later
说明寄信和收信之间经过了一段时间。
结尾用 was very happy to … 写人物感受，让故事自然收尾。

wrote a letter to her grandma = 给奶奶写了一封信
put a photo in the envelope = 把一张照片放进信封
postbox = 邮筒
posted it = 把它寄了出去
received the letter = 收到了信
was very happy to read it = 读信时很开心

注意过去式：write → wrote；put → put；go → went。
post → posted；receive → received；is → was。
write a letter to someone 表示“给某人写信”，注意 to。
was happy to 后用动词原形：read、see。
最后一句的 She 指奶奶，it 指信。
