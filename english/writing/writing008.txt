#title: Writing 008 | Story: A bike accident

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom and his friend were riding their bikes on a country
road. Suddenly, Tom fell off his bike and hurt his leg. His friend
called Tom's dad for help. Soon, his dad arrived by car to take
them home.

#tip

活动 → 意外 → 求助 → 帮助到来

Activity: Tom and his friend were riding their bikes on a country road.

Accident: Suddenly, Tom fell off his bike and hurt his leg.

Call for help: His friend called Tom's dad for help.

Help arrives: Soon, his dad arrived by car to take them home.

记住这个故事结构：Activity → Accident → Call for help → Help arrives

实用句块：
One day, … were riding their bikes.
Suddenly, … fell off his bike.
… hurt his leg.
… called … for help.
Soon, … arrived by car.
… to take them home.

这篇答案共 41 词，三幅图的关键内容都要写到：
两人骑车 → 一人摔倒、腿受伤，朋友打电话 → 大人开车来接。
把来接人的大人设定为 Tom 的爸爸，让人物关系简单清楚。
第二幅图不仅有“受伤”，还有“打电话”，写出求助能让故事衔接更完整。

用 One day 开始故事，Suddenly 引出意外，Soon 引出帮助到来。
country road = 乡间道路
fell off his bike = 从自行车上摔下来
hurt his leg = 伤了腿
called Tom's dad for help = 给 Tom 的爸爸打电话求助
arrived by car = 开车到达
to take them home = 为了带他们回家

注意时态：were riding 表示当时正在骑车；fell、hurt、called、arrived
表示随后发生的事情。
fall → fell；hurt 的过去式仍然是 hurt；take → took。
本篇 to take them home 中，to 后面用原形 take。

可以和 writing007 一起记：
007：called his friend and asked about it（打电话询问物品）
008：called Tom's dad for help（打电话求助）
同样是“打电话”，根据故事需要选择不同句块。
