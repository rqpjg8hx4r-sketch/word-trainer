#title: Writing 012 | Email: My favourite meal

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Robbie.

From: Robbie

When I got home from school yesterday, I really enjoyed the meal I ate with my family. What's your favourite meal? How often do you eat it? Who cooks it for you?

Write an email to Robbie and answer the questions.
Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Robbie,

Thanks for your email. My favourite meal is beef with rice. I usually eat it twice a week. My mum cooks it for me, and I love it because it is delicious.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

喜欢的餐食 → 吃的频率 → 谁来做

Favourite: My favourite meal is beef with rice.

Frequency: I usually eat it twice a week.

Cook: My mum cooks it for me.

记住这个邮件结构：Favourite → Frequency → Cook

写之前先找三个 task points：
What's your favourite meal? → beef with rice
How often do you eat it? → twice a week
Who cooks it for you? → my mum

每个任务点至少一句，优先保证 Content。
本题的第三个问题是“谁做饭”，不要只回答喜欢这道菜的原因。
I love it because it is delicious. 是回答完任务点后的补充。

实用句块：
Thanks for your email.
My favourite meal is …
I usually eat it …
My mum cooks it for me.
I love it because …

这篇正文共 32 词，包含称呼和署名共 36 词。
Thanks for your email. = 谢谢你的邮件，是自然的交流开场。
可以和已学的 Good to hear from you. 轮换使用，通常选一句即可。

beef with rice = 牛肉配米饭
twice a week = 每周两次
cooks it for me = 为我做这道菜
delicious = 美味的

频率句块可以根据自己的实际情况替换：
once a week = 每周一次
twice a week = 每周两次
three times a week = 每周三次
a few times a week = 每周几次

注意一般现在时：My mum cooks … 中 cook 要加 s。
cook something for someone：为某人做某道菜。
beef 和 rice 在这里都是不可数名词，不加 s。
Robbie 提到昨天的经历，但问的是你的喜好和习惯，所以回复用一般现在时。

这篇可以和 writing010、writing011 一起记：
010：Favourite → Story → Reason
011：Favourite → Reason → Cost
012：Favourite → Frequency → Cook
开头都能用 My favourite … is …，后面根据题目的任务点选择句块。
