#title: Writing 022 | Story: Joining a basketball game

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom was reading a book in the park. Two boys were playing basketball nearby. Suddenly, their ball came towards him, and he caught it. They asked him to join them. Soon, all three boys were playing together.

#tip

原来的活动 → 意外变化 → 邀请 → 一起玩

Activity: Tom was reading a book in the park.

Unexpected event: Their ball came towards him, and he caught it.

Invitation: They asked him to join them.

Joining in: Soon, all three boys were playing together.

记住这个故事结构：Activity → Unexpected event → Invitation → Joining in

这篇答案共 39 词，按三幅图的顺序写清楚：
一人读书、两人打篮球 → 球飞向读书的男孩，他接住球 → 三人一起打篮球。
把读书的男孩命名为 Tom，用 two boys 指另外两人。
They asked him to join them. 是连接接球和一起玩的合理补充。
球飞来不等于男孩受伤，本篇写 caught it，不添加受伤情节。

实用句块：
One day, … was reading a book.
Two boys were playing … nearby.
Suddenly, … came towards him.
He caught it.
They asked him to join them.
Soon, … were playing together.

nearby = 在附近
came towards him = 朝他过来
caught it = 接住了它
join them = 加入他们
all three boys = 三个男孩全都

注意过去式：come → came；catch → caught；ask → asked。
was reading 和 were playing 表示当时正在进行的活动。
Tom 是一人，用 was；two boys 和 all three boys 是多人，用 were。
ask someone to do something 表示“请某人做某事”：asked him to join them。
play basketball 中 basketball 前不加 a。

可以和 writing005 一起记：
005：球卡在树上，需要取下来。
022：球飞向读书的男孩，他接住球并加入游戏。
同样出现球，要根据图片的变化选择动作，不能照搬另一篇的情节。
