#title: Writing 010 | Email: My favourite film

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Jack.

From: Jack

I love watching films. What's your favourite film? What happens in the story? Why do you like it?

Write an email to Jack and answer the questions.
Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Jack,

Good to hear from you. My favourite film is Spider-Man. In the story, Peter Parker becomes a superhero and saves people. I love it because it is exciting and he is brave.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

电影名称 → 故事情节 → 喜欢的原因

Favourite: My favourite film is Spider-Man.

Story: In the story, Peter Parker becomes a superhero and saves people.

Reason: I love it because it is exciting and he is brave.

记住这个邮件结构：Favourite → Story → Reason

写之前先找三个 task points：
What's your favourite film? → Spider-Man
What happens in the story? → Peter Parker becomes a superhero and saves people.
Why do you like it? → It is exciting and he is brave.

每个任务点至少一句，优先保证 Content。
第二个问题要写故事中发生了什么，不能只写电影名称或“很好看”。
第三个问题要说明喜欢的原因，用 because 把理由写清楚。

实用句块：
Good to hear from you.
My favourite film is …
In the story, …
I love it because …

这篇正文共 32 词，包含称呼和署名共 36 词。
Good to hear from you. 是自然的交流开场，可以继续沿用 writing004 的句块。

becomes a superhero = 成为超级英雄
saves people = 救人
exciting = 令人兴奋的、精彩的
brave = 勇敢的

介绍电影情节时通常用一般现在时：becomes、saves。
Peter Parker 是单数，所以写 becomes 和 saves。
it 指代电影；he 指代 Peter Parker，避免重复名称。
形容电影用 exciting：The film is exciting.
形容自己的感受用 excited：I am excited.

沿用英式拼写 favourite，并注意片名 Spider-Man 的大小写和连字符。
这套结构也能用于介绍其他电影或书：替换名称、主要情节和喜欢的原因。
