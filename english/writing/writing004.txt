#title: Writing 004 | Email: My favourite sport

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Morgan.

I love doing sport! What’s your favourite sport? Why do you like doing
it? Where do you do it?

Write an email to Morgan and answer the questions. Write 25 words or
more.

Write the email on your answer sheet.

#answer

Hi Morgan,

Good to hear from you. My favourite sport is fencing. I love it because
it is fun and I am good at it. I go to the sports centre near my home to
practise fencing every week.

Cheers, 
Alex

#tip

最喜欢的运动 → 理由 → 地点 → 频率

Favourite: My favourite … is …

Reason: I love it because …

Place: I go to … to …

Frequency: … every week.

记住这个写作结构： Favourite → Reason → Place → Frequency

实用句型： My favourite sport is … I love it because … I go to … to
practise … … every week.

这个结构也可以用于 swimming、basketball、tennis 等类似的运动类邮件题。

EMAIL WRITING SUMMARY / 邮件写作总结

1) 四类已掌握结构
邀请 + 推荐：Invite → Suggest → Reason → End
回答喜好：Answer → Reason → Frequency → Friendly ending
邀请活动：Invite → When → Activity → Positive ending
回答运动：Favourite → Reason → Place → Frequency

2) 不背整篇，背可迁移的句块
根据题目替换关键词，灵活组合：
Would you like to ... with me?
How about ...?
We can ... together.
My favourite ... is ...
I like/love it because ...
I usually ... a few times a week.
I go to ... to ...
I am sure it will be fun.
Good to hear from you.
See you soon!
Cheers,

3) 写之前，先找三个 task points
先列出题目的三个任务点，每点至少写一句，优先保证 Content（内容完整、问题全部回答）。例如：favourite sport → why → where。写完逐项检查，避免遗漏。

4) 建立个人素材库
尽量使用孩子自己的真实信息，例如 Eggy Party、fencing、sports centre near my home。积累真实、熟悉、容易记住的素材，遇到新题目时灵活调用。

5) 推荐写作目标
30–45 words；覆盖全部任务点；使用 1–2 个连接词；基本零语法错误；再加一句自然的人际交流，例如 Good to hear from you. 或 I am sure it will be fun.
优先使用自己熟练掌握的表达。

6) 下一阶段：陌生题目训练
先只看题目，不看范文，独立写 30–40 词。之后只修改真正有问题的地方，尽量保留自己的表达，逐渐从“学范文”走向“会写”。
