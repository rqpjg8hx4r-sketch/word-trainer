#title: Writing 011 | Email: My favourite music website

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Andi.

From: Andi

I want to find a good website for listening to music. What is your favourite music website? Why do you like it? How much does it cost to use?

Write an email to Andi and answer the questions.
Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Andi,

Good to hear from you. My favourite music website is QQ Music. I like it because it has lots of songs and is easy to use. It costs eight yuan a month.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

网站名称 → 喜欢的原因 → 费用

Favourite: My favourite music website is QQ Music.

Reason: I like it because it has lots of songs and is easy to use.

Cost: It costs eight yuan a month.

记住这个邮件结构：Favourite → Reason → Cost

写之前先找三个 task points：
What is your favourite music website? → QQ Music
Why do you like it? → lots of songs + easy to use
How much does it cost to use? → eight yuan a month

每个任务点至少一句，优先保证 Content。
写费用时同时说明金额和时间单位，让“每月 8 元”表达完整。

实用句块：
Good to hear from you.
My favourite … is …
I like it because …
It has lots of …
It is easy to use.
It costs … a month.

这篇正文共 32 词，包含称呼和署名共 36 词。
费用按本题给定的每月 8 元写作 eight yuan a month。
用 because 解释原因，用 and 连接“歌曲多”和“容易使用”两个理由。
Good to hear from you. 保留自然的交流开场。

lots of songs = 很多歌曲
easy to use = 容易使用
eight yuan a month = 每月八元

注意一般现在时：it has、it is、it costs。
It costs … 中 cost 要加 s，不能写 It cost …。
can 后面用动词原形，例如 You can listen to music.
listen to music 中不要漏掉 to。
yuan 表示人民币元，这里不用加 s。

这篇可以和 writing010 一起记：
010：Favourite → Story → Reason
011：Favourite → Reason → Cost
先找清三个任务点，再调用已掌握的句块，不要把不同题目的问题混在一起。
