#title: Writing 019 | Email: A cycle race

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Chris.

From: Chris

Please tell me about the cycle race on Saturday. Where is it? What time does it begin? What do I need to bring?

Write an email to Chris and answer the questions.
Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Chris,

Thanks for your email. The cycle race is in the park near my home. It begins at nine in the morning on Saturday. Please bring your bike, a helmet and some water.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

比赛地点 → 开始时间 → 携带物品

Place: The cycle race is in the park near my home.

Start time: It begins at nine in the morning on Saturday.

Things to bring: Please bring your bike, a helmet and some water.

记住这个邮件结构：Place → Start time → Things to bring

三个 task points：
Where is it? → in the park near my home
What time does it begin? → at nine in the morning on Saturday
What do I need to bring? → your bike + a helmet + some water

这篇正文共 32 词，包含称呼和署名共 36 词。
Thanks for your email. 是自然的回复开场。
时间写成“星期六上午九点”，让安排清楚具体。
携带物品要符合自行车比赛的活动内容。

实用句块：
Thanks for your email.
The … is in the park near my home.
It begins at …
Please bring …

cycle race = 自行车比赛
near my home = 在我家附近
a helmet = 一个头盔
in the morning = 在上午

It begins … 中 begin 要加 s。
虽然比赛还没有举行，固定安排的开始时间可以用一般现在时表达。
具体钟点用 at：at nine；星期几用 on：on Saturday。
上午用 in the morning，也可以把 at nine in the morning 写成 at 9 a.m.

和 writing009 对比：
009：Destination → Things to take → Return time
019：Place → Start time → Things to bring
注意区分“几点到家”和“几点开始”，根据题目选择时间句块。
