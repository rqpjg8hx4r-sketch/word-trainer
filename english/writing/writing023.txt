#title: Writing 023 | Story: Finding treasure

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom and Anna looked at a treasure map. They followed it to a big tree. Tom found some coins inside the tree. They were very excited to find the treasure and smiled at each other.

#tip

看地图 → 按地图寻找 → 发现宝藏 → 感受

Map: Tom and Anna looked at a treasure map.

Search: They followed it to a big tree.

Discovery: Tom found some coins inside the tree.

Feeling: They were very excited and smiled at each other.

记住这个故事结构：Map → Search → Discovery → Feeling

这篇答案共 37 词，三幅图的主要内容都要写到：
两人看地图 → 来到大树旁，从树洞里找东西 → 找到硬币组成的宝藏，很开心。
把男孩命名为 Tom，把女孩命名为 Anna，方便区分人物。
地图上的树与第二幅图的大树相对应，followed it 把看地图和寻找连接起来。

实用句块：
One day, … looked at a map.
They followed it to …
… found … inside …
They were very excited to …
They smiled at each other.

a treasure map = 一张藏宝图
followed it to a big tree = 按着地图来到一棵大树旁
some coins = 一些硬币
inside the tree = 在树里面，这里指树洞里
treasure = 宝藏
smiled at each other = 相视而笑

注意过去式：look → looked；follow → followed；find → found；smile → smiled。
look at a map 中不要漏掉 at。
followed it 中 it 指代地图；按照地图行动让故事顺序清楚。
were excited to 后用动词原形：find。
excited 表示人的感受；exciting 表示事情令人兴奋。
coins 是可数名词复数；treasure 在本篇中是不可数名词。

可以和 writing013 一起记：
013：was happy to read it（开心地读信）
023：were excited to find the treasure（找到宝藏很兴奋）
用“感受 + to + 动词”让故事自然收尾。
