#title: Writing 003 | Email: A sports centre invitation

#question

You would like to go to the sports centre with your friend, Sam. Write
an email to Sam.

In your email: - invite Sam to the sports centre - say when you would
like to go - suggest an activity you can do there.

Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Sam,

How are you today? Would you like to go to the sports centre with me
this Sunday? How about 2 p.m.? We can swim together. I am sure it will
be fun.

See you soon! 
Alex

#tip

邀请 → 时间 → 活动 → 积极结束

Invite: Would you like to go to … with me?

When: How about …?

Activity: We can … together.

Positive ending: I am sure it will be fun.

记住这个写作结构： Invite → When → Activity → Positive ending

实用句型： How about + 时间？

例如： How about Saturday afternoon? How about 10 a.m.? How about next
Sunday?

这个结构也可以用于 sports centre、cinema、party、picnic
等类似的邀请邮件题。
