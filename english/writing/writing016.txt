#title: Writing 016 | Story: A dance show

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

Anna practised dancing with her friends for a show. On the day of the show, they danced on stage in beautiful dresses. Afterwards, her dad gave her some flowers. She was very happy, and her friends smiled.

#tip

练习 → 表演 → 收到鲜花 → 感受

Practice: Anna practised dancing with her friends for a show.

Performance: They danced on stage in beautiful dresses.

Flowers: Afterwards, her dad gave her some flowers.

Feeling: She was very happy, and her friends smiled.

记住这个故事结构：Practice → Performance → Flowers → Feeling

实用句块：
… practised dancing with her friends.
On the day of the show, …
They danced on stage.
Afterwards, … gave her some flowers.
She was very happy.

这篇答案共 37 词，按三幅图的顺序写清楚：
女孩们一起练习舞蹈 → 穿着漂亮裙子上台表演 → 一位大人送来鲜花。
把主要女孩命名为 Anna，把送花的大人设定为她的爸爸，让人物关系简单清楚。
第三幅图出现的是鲜花，不必把表演写成“赢得比赛”或“获得奖杯”。

practised dancing = 练习跳舞
for a show = 为一场演出
on the day of the show = 在演出当天
on stage = 在舞台上
in beautiful dresses = 穿着漂亮裙子
afterwards = 之后
gave her some flowers = 给了她一些鲜花

注意过去式：practise → practised；dance → danced；give → gave；smile → smiled。
沿用英式拼写 practise；practise 后接动词时用 -ing：practised dancing。
in + 衣服表示“穿着……”，例如 in beautiful dresses。
girls 是多人，dresses 也用复数，表示她们各自穿着裙子。

这篇可以和 writing014 一起记：
014：His brother gave him a football.
016：Her dad gave her some flowers.
同一个 gave someone something 句型，可以替换人物和物品，灵活使用。
