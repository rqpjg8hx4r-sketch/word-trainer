#title: Writing 005 | Story: A football stuck in a tree

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, two friends were playing football in a park. Suddenly, the
ball got stuck in a tree. They looked up at it. Then one of them
climbed the tree to get the ball. The other friend waited below.

#tip

活动 → 问题 → 反应 → 行动

Activity: Two friends were playing football in a park.

Problem: Suddenly, the ball got stuck in a tree.

Reaction: They looked up at it.

Action: One of them climbed the tree to get the ball.

记住这个故事结构：Activity → Problem → Reaction → Action

实用句块：
One day, … were playing …
Suddenly, … got stuck in …
They looked up at …
Then one of them …
… to get the ball.
The other friend waited below.

这篇答案共 39 词，使用短句，按图片顺序讲故事。
每幅图都要写到：踢球 → 球卡在树上、两人抬头看 → 一人爬树取球。
用 One day、Suddenly、Then 连接情节，注意保持过去时。
were playing 表示当时正在进行的活动；got、looked、climbed、waited
表示随后发生的事情。

got stuck in a tree 表示“卡在树上”，比 fell on the tree 更贴切。
climbed the tree to get the ball 表示“爬树去取球”，to get 说明目的。
one of them / the other friend 表示“其中一人 / 另一位朋友”。

结尾写到第三幅图中的爬树取球即可，不必补写图中没有出现的“他们继续踢球”，也不必添加“他们刚认识”等背景。
