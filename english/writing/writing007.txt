#title: Writing 007 | Story: Getting a book back

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom could not find his favourite book. He called his friend
and asked about it. Luckily, his friend had the book. Later, his
friend came to his house by bike and gave it back. Tom thanked him.

#tip

遇到问题 → 联系朋友 → 解决问题 → 感谢

Problem: Tom could not find his favourite book.

Phone call: He called his friend and asked about it.

Solution: His friend had the book and later gave it back.

Thanks: Tom thanked him.

记住这个故事结构：Problem → Phone call → Solution → Thanks

实用句块：
One day, … could not find …
He called his friend and asked about …
Luckily, … had …
Later, … came to his house by bike.
… gave it back.
… thanked him.

这篇答案共 39 词，按图片顺序写清楚：
找不到书 → 打电话询问朋友 → 朋友骑车送还书。
最后加一句 Tom thanked him.，让故事自然收尾。

用 One day 开始故事，Luckily 引出好消息，Later 连接后续行动。
无需添加书名，也不必解释朋友什么时候借走了书。

could not find his favourite book = 找不到他最喜欢的书
called his friend = 给朋友打电话
asked about it = 询问这本书的情况
had the book = 手里有这本书
came to his house by bike = 骑车来到他家
gave it back = 把它还了回来
thanked him = 向他道谢

注意过去式：come → came；give → gave；thank → thanked。
could not 后用动词原形：could not find。
give back 后面的宾语如果是 it，要放在中间：give it back / gave it back。
用 it 指代前面提到的书，避免反复写 the book。
