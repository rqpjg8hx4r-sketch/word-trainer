#title: Writing 006 | Story: Winning a bike race

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom and three friends took part in a bike race. At first,
he was behind the others. But he did not give up and rode faster.
In the end, he won the race. Everyone cheered for him.

#tip

参加活动 → 遇到困难 → 努力 → 结果

Activity: Tom and three friends took part in a bike race.

Difficulty: At first, he was behind the others.

Effort: But he did not give up and rode faster.

Result: In the end, he won the race. Everyone cheered for him.

记住这个故事结构：Activity → Difficulty → Effort → Result

实用句块：
One day, … took part in …
At first, … was behind the others.
But … did not give up.
In the end, … won the race.
Everyone cheered for …

这篇答案共 39 词，三幅图的主要内容都要写到：
四人参加自行车比赛 → Tom 落在其他人后面 → Tom 最后获胜，观众欢呼。
“没有放弃、骑得更快”是连接落后与获胜的合理补充。

用 At first、But、In the end 写清楚“开始怎样、如何转折、最后怎样”。
不必添加“其他人累了”等图片中无法确定的原因。

took part in a bike race = 参加自行车比赛
was behind the others = 落在其他人后面
did not give up = 没有放弃
rode faster = 骑得更快
won the race = 赢得比赛
cheered for him = 为他欢呼

注意过去式：take → took；ride → rode；win → won。
did not 后用动词原形：did not give up。

和 writing005 对比：
005：Activity → Problem → Reaction → Action
006：Activity → Difficulty → Effort → Result
