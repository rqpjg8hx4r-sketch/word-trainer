#title: Writing 014 | Story: A birthday present

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

It was Tom's birthday. He celebrated with his family and had a birthday cake. His brother gave him a football as a present. Later, they went to the park and played football together. Tom was very happy.

#tip

生日庆祝 → 收到礼物 → 使用礼物 → 感受

Celebration: He celebrated with his family and had a birthday cake.

Present: His brother gave him a football as a present.

Activity: Later, they went to the park and played football together.

Feeling: Tom was very happy.

记住这个故事结构：Celebration → Present → Activity → Feeling

实用句块：
It was …'s birthday.
… celebrated with his family.
… gave him … as a present.
Later, they … together.
… was very happy.

这篇答案共 37 词，按三幅图的顺序写清楚：
和家人一起庆祝生日 → 收到足球 → 和送礼物的人一起踢球。
把收礼物的男孩命名为 Tom，把送礼物的人设定为他的兄弟，让人物关系简单清楚。
足球连接了第二幅和第三幅图，要写清楚礼物是什么以及后来做了什么。

celebrated with his family = 和家人一起庆祝
gave him a football = 给了他一个足球
as a present = 作为礼物
played football together = 一起踢足球

注意过去式：give → gave；go → went；have → had；is → was。
play → played；celebrate → celebrated。
gave someone something 表示“给某人某物”，例如 His brother gave him a football.
play football 中 football 前不加 a。
It was Tom's birthday. 是过去发生的生日故事的自然开头。

可以和 writing013 一起记：
013：信和照片连接前后的情节。
014：足球连接收到礼物和使用礼物的情节。
看图时留意重复出现的物品，它们能帮助你把故事串起来。
