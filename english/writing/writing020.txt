#title: Writing 020 | Email: An art group

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Casey.

From: Casey

Thanks for asking me to go with you to your art group on Saturday. What time does it start? What do I need to bring? How much does it cost?

Write an email to Casey and answer the questions.
Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Casey,

Thanks for your email. The art group starts at ten in the morning on Saturday. Please bring some paper and coloured pencils. It costs ten yuan for each person. I am sure you will enjoy it.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

开始时间 → 携带物品 → 费用 → 友好结尾

Start time: The art group starts at ten in the morning on Saturday.

Things to bring: Please bring some paper and coloured pencils.

Cost: It costs ten yuan for each person.

Friendly ending: I am sure you will enjoy it.

记住这个邮件结构：Start time → Things to bring → Cost → Friendly ending

三个 task points：
What time does it start? → at ten in the morning on Saturday
What do I need to bring? → some paper + coloured pencils
How much does it cost? → ten yuan for each person

这篇正文共 36 词，包含称呼和署名共 40 词。
本篇把活动安排在星期六上午十点，费用设定为每人十元。
Casey 已经答应一起去，直接回答安排即可，无需再次邀请。
最后一句 I am sure you will enjoy it. 让邮件自然收尾。

实用句块：
Thanks for your email.
The … starts at …
Please bring …
It costs … for each person.
I am sure you will enjoy it.

art group = 美术小组
some paper = 一些纸
coloured pencils = 彩色铅笔
for each person = 每人

注意一般现在时：starts、costs 都要加 s。
will 后用动词原形：will enjoy。
paper 表示纸张时是不可数名词，不写 some papers。
沿用英式拼写 coloured；yuan 在这里不加 s。

和 writing011 一起记：
011：It costs eight yuan a month.（每月费用）
020：It costs ten yuan for each person.（每人费用）
金额之外，还要根据活动说明费用的单位。
