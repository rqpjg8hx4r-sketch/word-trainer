#title: Writing 024 | Story: Singing at a concert

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

Anna and her friend went to a concert. They enjoyed listening to their favourite singer. During the show, he invited them onto the stage. They sang with him in front of everyone. It was a very exciting evening.

#tip

去看演出 → 欣赏歌手 → 受邀上台 → 一起唱歌 → 感受

Arrival: Anna and her friend went to a concert.

Show: They enjoyed listening to their favourite singer.

Invitation: He invited them onto the stage.

Participation: They sang with him in front of everyone.

Feeling: It was a very exciting evening.

记住这个故事结构：Arrival → Show → Invitation → Participation → Feeling

这篇答案共 38 词，按三幅图的顺序写清楚：
两个女孩来到演出场所 → 看歌手演唱 → 和歌手一起在舞台上唱歌。
把其中一个女孩命名为 Anna，用 her friend 指另一人。
歌手邀请她们上台是连接第二幅和第三幅图的合理补充。
最重要的变化是从看演出到参与演出，要明确写出 sang with him。

实用句块：
… went to a concert.
They enjoyed listening to …
During the show, …
He invited them onto the stage.
They sang with him.
It was a very exciting evening.

a concert = 一场演唱会
their favourite singer = 她们最喜欢的歌手
during the show = 在演出期间
onto the stage = 到舞台上
in front of everyone = 在所有人面前
an exciting evening = 一个令人兴奋的夜晚

注意过去式：go → went；enjoy → enjoyed；invite → invited；sing → sang。
enjoy 后接动词时用 -ing：enjoyed listening。
listen to someone 中不要漏掉 to。
onto the stage 表示移动到舞台上；on stage 表示在舞台上。
exciting 形容这个夜晚；如果写人物感受，用 They were excited.

和 writing016 对比：
016：先练习舞蹈，再上台表演，最后收到鲜花。
024：先看歌手演出，再受邀上台一起唱歌。
两篇都有舞台，但要写清各自图片中的情节变化。
