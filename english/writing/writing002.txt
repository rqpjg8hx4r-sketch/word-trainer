#title: Writing 002 | Email: My favourite phone game

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Spencer.

I’ve just got a great new game on my phone. It’s called SurfzUp. What’s
your favourite game on your phone? Why do you like it? How often do you
play it?

Write an email to Spencer and answer the questions. Write 25 words or
more.

Write the email on your answer sheet.

#answer

Hi Spencer,

My favourite game on my phone is Eggy Party. I like it very much because
it is full of fun and I can play it with my friends online. I usually
play it a few times a week. We can play it together sometime.

Best wishes, 
Alex

#tip

回答 → because 给理由 → 频率 → 友好结束

Answer: My favourite … is …

Give a reason: I like it because …

Frequency: I usually … a few times a week.

Friendly ending: We can … together sometime.

记住这个写作结构： Answer → Reason → Frequency → Friendly ending

这个结构也可以用于 favourite sport、TV programme、activity、app
等类似的邮件题。
