#title: Writing 015 | Story: A family holiday

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom and his parents planned a holiday at the beach. They looked at pictures of sunny beaches together. Later, they got on a plane in the rain. When they arrived, it was sunny. They were happy to be on holiday.

#tip

计划旅行 → 乘飞机出发 → 到达目的地 → 感受

Plan: Tom and his parents planned a holiday at the beach.

Departure: Later, they got on a plane in the rain.

Arrival: When they arrived, it was sunny.

Feeling: They were happy to be on holiday.

记住这个故事结构：Plan → Departure → Arrival → Feeling

实用句块：
One day, … planned a holiday.
They looked at pictures of …
Later, they got on a plane.
When they arrived, …
They were happy to …

这篇答案共 42 词，三幅图的主要内容都要写到：
一家人看旅行资料、计划度假 → 下雨时登机 → 来到晴朗的海边。
把孩子命名为 Tom，把两位大人设定为他的父母，让人物关系简单清楚。
第一幅图可以用 looked at pictures 简单表达，不必记复杂的旅行资料词汇。
第二幅图和第三幅图的天气不同，写出 in the rain 和 it was sunny 能让细节更完整。

planned a holiday = 计划一次度假
looked at pictures of sunny beaches = 看晴朗海滩的图片
got on a plane = 登上飞机
in the rain = 在雨中
when they arrived = 当他们到达时
be on holiday = 在度假

注意过去式：plan → planned；get → got；arrive → arrived。
plan 的过去式要双写 n，再加 ed。
They were … 中主语是复数，所以用 were；It was sunny. 中用 was。
When they arrived, it was sunny. 用 when 把到达和天气连接起来。
沿用英式表达 holiday；by plane 表示“乘飞机”，get on a plane 表示“登机”。

旅行故事可以按“计划、出发、到达、感受”组织。
不必补写具体国家、航班时间或图片中没有出现的活动。
