#title: Writing 021 | Story: Money on the street

#question

Look at the three pictures.
Write the story shown in the pictures.
Write 35 words or more.

#answer

One day, Tom and his friend were walking down the street. They saw some money on the ground. Tom picked it up. Then they went to an ice cream shop and bought two ice creams. They were very happy.

#tip

发现 → 拾起 → 购买 → 感受

Discovery: They saw some money on the ground.

Action: Tom picked it up.

Purchase: They went to an ice cream shop and bought two ice creams.

Feeling: They were very happy.

记住这个故事结构：Discovery → Action → Purchase → Feeling

这篇答案共 39 词，三幅图的关键内容都要写到：
两个男孩看到地上的钱 → 其中一人把钱捡起来 → 两人去店里买冰淇淋。
把捡钱的男孩命名为 Tom，用 his friend 指另一人，人物关系简单清楚。
题目是按图讲故事，结尾写到购买冰淇淋即可。

实用句块：
One day, … were walking down the street.
They saw … on the ground.
… picked it up.
Then they went to … and bought …
They were very happy.

walk down the street = 沿着街道走
on the ground = 在地上
picked it up = 把它捡起来
an ice cream shop = 一家冰淇淋店
two ice creams = 两份冰淇淋

注意过去式：see → saw；go → went；buy → bought。
pick → picked；is → was；are → were。
were walking 表示当时正在进行的活动，后面用 saw、picked、went、bought 讲后续动作。
pick up 的宾语如果是 it，要放在中间：picked it up。
money 是不可数名词，用 some money，不加 s。
ice cream 表示食物时通常不可数；two ice creams 可以表示两份冰淇淋。

可以和 writing007 一起记：
007：gave it back（把它还回来）
021：picked it up（把它捡起来）
这两个句块中的 it 都放在动词和后面的小词之间。
