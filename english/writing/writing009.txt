#title: Writing 009 | Email: A Saturday bike ride

#question

Read the email from your English friend, Daniel.

From: Daniel

Thanks for asking me to go on a bike ride with you on Saturday. Where shall we go? What shall we take with us? What time do you think we'll get home?

Write an email to Daniel and answer the questions.
Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Daniel,

Let's ride to the park near my home on Saturday. We can take some water and sandwiches with us. I think we will get home at about four o'clock. I am sure it will be fun.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

目的地 → 携带物品 → 到家时间 → 友好结尾

Destination: Let's ride to the park near my home on Saturday.

Things to take: We can take some water and sandwiches with us.

Return time: I think we will get home at about four o'clock.

Friendly ending: I am sure it will be fun.

记住这个邮件结构：
Destination → Things to take → Return time → Friendly ending

写之前先找三个 task points：
Where shall we go? → the park near my home
What shall we take with us? → some water and sandwiches
What time do you think we'll get home? → at about four o'clock

每个任务点至少一句，优先保证 Content，不要遗漏问题。
What shall we take with us? 问的是“带什么物品”，不能只回答和谁一起去。

实用句块：
Let's ride to …
We can take … with us.
I think we will get home at about …
I am sure it will be fun.

这篇正文共 36 词，包含称呼和署名共 40 词。
地点、物品和时间都很具体，最后一句让邮件更像真实交流。
Daniel 已经接受邀请，直接安排活动即可，无需再次邀请。

Let's 后用动词原形：Let's ride to …
We can 后用动词原形：We can take …
I think we will … 用来表达对未来的预计。
get home = 到家，home 前不加 to。
at about four o'clock = 大约四点；也可以写 at about 4 p.m.

这个结构也能用于野餐、去海边等活动：替换目的地、携带物品和预计到家时间，再根据题目选择合适的出行方式。
