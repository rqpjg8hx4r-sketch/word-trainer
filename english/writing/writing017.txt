#title: Writing 017 | Email: A camping invitation

#question

You are going camping next weekend with your family.
Write an email to your English friend, Lee.

In your email:
- ask Lee to come camping with you and your family
- say why you are going camping
- tell Lee what to bring.

Write 25 words or more.

#answer

Hi Lee,

Would you like to go camping with me and my family next weekend? We are going because we love spending time outdoors. Please bring a sleeping bag and some warm clothes. I am sure it will be fun.

Cheers,
Alex

#tip

邀请 → 露营的原因 → 携带物品 → 友好结尾

Invite: Would you like to go camping with me and my family next weekend?

Reason: We are going because we love spending time outdoors.

Things to bring: Please bring a sleeping bag and some warm clothes.

Friendly ending: I am sure it will be fun.

记住这个邮件结构：Invite → Reason → Things to bring → Friendly ending

三个 task points：
邀请 Lee 和你及家人一起露营 → Would you like to … with me and my family?
为什么去露营 → We love spending time outdoors.
需要带什么 → a sleeping bag + some warm clothes

这篇正文共 38 词，包含称呼和署名共 42 词。
题目给定的 next weekend 和 with your family 要保留在邀请里。
原因要说明你们为什么去露营，不能只列出露营时要做的活动。

实用句块：
Would you like to … with me and my family?
We are going because …
Please bring …
I am sure it will be fun.

go camping = 去露营
spend time outdoors = 在户外度过时间
a sleeping bag = 一个睡袋
some warm clothes = 一些暖和的衣服

Would you like to 后用动词原形：go。
love 后可以接 -ing：love spending time outdoors。
Please bring … 是简单自然的携带物品提示。
这篇是主动邀请，可以直接用 Would you like to … 开场。
