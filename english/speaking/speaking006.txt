# Day: 5
# Title: 今日口语作业

Q1: Is there a sports centre near your home?
A1: Yes, there is. It is about fifteen minutes walk from my home. I often go there for fencing practice, play badminton with my friends.

Q2: How long is your lunch break?
A2: My lunch break is about one hour. I usually have lunch at school, and finish my morning homework in the noon time. Sometimes I take a nap after that.

Q3: Tell me something about an animal that you like.
A3: My like cat the best.I have a two years old cat, it's name is Lulu. Lulu is a British Shorthair. Lulu eats a lot, so it is fat and lazy, but I still love it.
