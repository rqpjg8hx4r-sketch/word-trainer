Q: Do you like these different sports? Say why or why not.

A: There are five different sports: tennis, basketball, table tennis,
skiing, and football.

I don’t like tennis because it is difficult for me.

I like basketball because it is exciting and I can play it with my
friends.

I like table tennis because it is fun and easy to play.

I like skiing very much because it is exciting. I went skiing with my
mom during my last winter holiday.

I don’t like football because I am not very good at it.
