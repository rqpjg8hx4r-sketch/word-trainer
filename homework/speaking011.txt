# Day: 11
# Title: Speaking Practice — Sophie's Example
# Privacy: All names, dates, addresses, schools and class details below are fictional examples.

# Greeting and introduction

Q1: How do you do?
A1: How do you do?

Q2: How are you today?
A2: Great, thanks. And what about you?

Q3: It is a fine day today, isn't it?
A3: Yes, it is.

Q4: How is the weather today?
A4: It is a sunny day today. It is a cloudy day today. It is a snowy day today. It is a windy day today.

Q5: What day is it today?
A5: Today is Monday. Today is Tuesday. Today is Wednesday. Today is Thursday. Today is Friday. Today is Saturday. Today is Sunday.

Q6: What is the date today?
A6: Today is the first of January. Today is the second of February. Today is the third of March. Today is the fourth of April.

Q7: How did you get here this morning?
A7: I got here by bus.

Q8: What is your name?
A8: My name is Sophie Lee. Lee is my family name, and Sophie is my given name.

Q9: May I know your name?
A9: Sure. My name is Sophie Lee.

Q10: Can you spell your name, please?
A10: S-O-P-H-I-E, Sophie. L-E-E, Lee.

Q11: What is your full name?
A11: My full name is Sophie Lee.

Q12: What is your surname?
A12: My surname is Lee. L-E-E, Lee.

Q13: What is your family name?
A13: My family name is Lee. L-E-E, Lee.

Q14: Do you have an English name?
A14: Yes. My English name is Sophie.

Q15: What is your English name?
A15: My English name is Sophie.

Q16: When is your birthday?
A16: My birthday is on the tenth of May.

Q17: How old are you?
A17: I am eleven years old.

Q18: When were you born?
A18: I was born on the tenth of May, 2015.

Q19: Where were you born?
A19: I was born in New York City.

Q20: Were you born in London?
A20: No, I was born in New York City.

Q21: Were you born in this city?
A21: Yes, I was born in this city.

Q22: Are you a local resident?
A22: Yes, I have lived here for eleven years.

Q23: Are you a native of this city?
A23: Yes, I was born here and have always lived here.

Q24: Where are you from?
A24: I am from New York City. It is a big city.

Q25: Where do you come from?
A25: I come from New York City. It is a big city.

Q26: Where is your birthplace?
A26: My birthplace is New York City.

Q27: Where do you live?
A27: I live at Number 10 New York Drive, New York City.

Q28: Do you live in this city?
A28: Yes, I live in this city.

# About school

Q29: Which school do you go to?
A29: I go to Sunshine Primary School.

Q30: Which grade are you in?
A30: I am in Grade 4.

Q31: Is your home far from your school?
A31: No. It is only two stops by bus, or a five-minute drive.

Q32: How far is your home from your school?
A32: It is about two kilometres away.

Q33: How long does it take you to get to school?
A33: It takes me fifteen minutes by bus.

Q34: When do you go to school every day?
A34: I usually go to school at half past seven.

Q35: How do you get to school every day?
A35: I usually go to school by bus. Sometimes I go in my parents' car.

Q36: Which class are you in?
A36: I am in Class 2, Grade 4.

Q37: How many students are there in your class?
A37: There are thirty students in my class: sixteen boys and fourteen girls.

Q38: How many boys and girls are there in your class?
A38: There are sixteen boys and fourteen girls in my class.

Q39: Do you like your school?
A39: Yes, I do. My school has nice teachers and friendly students.

Q40: Do you like your class?
A40: Yes, I do. My teacher is nice, and my classmates are friendly.

Q41: What do you think of your school?
A41: I think my school is great. It has a beautiful campus and nice teachers.

Q42: What do you think of your class?
A42: I think my class is great. My teacher is nice, and my classmates are friendly.

Q43: What is your school like?
A43: My school is great. It has a beautiful campus and nice teachers.

Q44: What is your class like?
A44: My class is friendly and full of energy.

Q45: What does your school look like?
A45: My school looks beautiful. It has a large playground and many trees.

Q46: What does your classroom look like?
A46: My classroom looks bright and welcoming.

Q47: Is there a library at your school?
A47: Yes, there is. I sometimes borrow books from the school library.

Q48: Does your school have a Reading Club?
A48: Yes, it does. I am a member of the Reading Club, and I can read many books there.

Q49: Does your school have an English Corner?
A49: Yes, it does. I sometimes go there to practise speaking English with other students.

Q50: What do you usually do at the English Corner?
A50: I usually talk with other students and practise speaking English.

Q51: When does the first lesson start?
A51: The first lesson starts at eight o'clock.

Q52: When do you go home from school every day?
A52: I go home from school at four o'clock every day.

Q53: What do you usually do between classes?
A53: I usually play games with my friends between classes.

# About subjects

Q54: How many subjects do you have? What are they?
A54: I have three main subjects: Chinese, Maths and English. I also have Music, Art and PE, which means Physical Education.

Q55: What subjects do you have?
A55: I have Chinese, Maths, English, Music, Art and PE.

Q56: What is your favourite subject? Why do you like it best?
A56: My favourite subject is English because it helps me understand the world better.

Q57: What do you think of English? Is it easy or difficult?
A57: English is interesting, and regular practice makes it easier.

Q58: Is English hard or easy for you?
A58: English is easy for me because I practise it often.

Q59: Do you like learning English?
A59: Yes, I do. I like learning English because it helps me understand the world better.

Q60: What is your English teacher's name?
A60: My English teacher's name is Ms Green. She is a nice teacher.

Q61: Why do you like English?
A61: I like English because it helps me understand the wider world better.

Q62: Why do you like Maths?
A62: I like Maths because I enjoy solving problems.

Q63: Why do you like Chinese?
A63: I like Chinese because there are many interesting stories in Chinese.

Q64: Are you good at English?
A64: Yes, I am. I usually get an A in English tests.

Q65: Do you do well in PE?
A65: Yes, I do. I am good at basketball.

Q66: Which subject are you good at?
A66: I am good at English because I practise it every day.

Q67: Are you tired after lessons?
A67: Yes, I am. I usually rest for about thirty minutes after I get home.

Q68: What do you do after school?
A68: I usually go straight home and do my homework.

Q69: Do you read English every day?
A69: No, I read English every other day.

Q70: Do you like sports?
A70: Yes, I do. I especially like basketball.

Q71: What sport do you like best? Why do you like it, and how often do you play it?
A71: I like basketball best because it is exciting. I play it every Tuesday and Saturday.
