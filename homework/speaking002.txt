# Day: 2
# Title: 今日口语作业

Q1: Which season do you like best?
A1: I like winter best because it is cold and quiet. I can play in the snow and on the ice. During my last winter holiday, I went skiing with my mom.

Q2: What do you usually do in your spare time?
A2: I usually play games such as Mini World and Eggy Party. They are fun, and I have made a lot of friends online.

Q3: How does your family spend the weekend?
A3: My family usually plays cards at home. Sometimes we play sports. This weekend, we will play badminton together.
