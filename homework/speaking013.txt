Q: Do you like reading these different books? Say why or why not.

A: There are five different kinds of magazines: computer magazines,
fashion magazines, travel magazines, music magazines, and science
magazines.

I like reading computer magazines because I can learn about new
technology.

I don’t like reading fashion magazines because I am not interested in
fashion.

I like reading travel magazines because I can learn about interesting
places around the world.

I like reading music magazines because I enjoy listening to music.

I like reading science magazines because I can learn many interesting
things about science.
