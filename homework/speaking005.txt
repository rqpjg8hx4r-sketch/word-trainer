# Day: 5
# Title: 今日口语作业

Q1: What is your favourite subject? Why do you like it best?
A1: My favourite subject is mathematics. I like it best because it is interesting and useful. I enjoy solving maths problems.

Q2: What do you do after school every day?
A2: I usually do my homework after school. Then I practice fencing for about two hours. Sometimes I go swimming with my mom.

Q3: What did you do last Mid-Autumn Festival?
A3: Last Mid-Autumn Festival, I had dinner with my family. We ate mooncakes and enjoyed the beautiful full moon together. We had a great time.
