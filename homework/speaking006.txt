# Day: 5
# Title: 今日口语作业

Q1: Is there a sports centre near your home?
A1: Yes, there is. It is about fifteen minutes from my home. I often go there for fencing practice and play on the trampoline with my friends.

Q2: How long is your lunch break?
A2: My lunch break is about one hour. I usually have lunch at school, finish my morning homework, and prepare for my afternoon classes. Sometimes I take a short rest after lunch.

Q3: Tell me something about an animal that you like.
A3: My favourite animal is the cat. I have a cat called Lulu. He is a British Shorthair. He is cute and friendly. I like playing with him because it makes me feel happy and relaxed.
