Q: Do you like these different types of online programmes? Say why or
why not.

A: There are five different types of online programmes: cartoons, sports
programmes, educational programmes, news programmes, and animal
programmes.

I like cartoons because they are funny and interesting. They make me
happy.

I like sports programmes because I like sports. I can learn some new
skills from them.

I like educational programmes because I can learn a lot of new things
from them.

I don’t really like news programmes because sometimes they are a little
boring for me.

I like animal programmes very much because I love animals. I can learn a
lot about different animals from them.
