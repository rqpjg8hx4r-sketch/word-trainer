Q: Do you like eating in these places? Say why or why not.

A: There are five different places to eat: a restaurant, a fast-food
restaurant, a café, a picnic area, and the school canteen.

I like eating in a restaurant because the food is delicious and I can
enjoy it with my family.

I like eating in a fast-food restaurant because I like hamburgers and
French fries.

I like eating in a café because it is quiet and relaxing.

I like having a picnic because I can enjoy good food and fresh air with
my friends.

I like eating in the school canteen because it is convenient and I can
have lunch with my classmates.
