# Day: 3
# Title: 今日口语作业

Q1: Please tell me something about your favourite time of the year.
A1: My favourite time of the year is the winter holiday. I stay with my family and celebrate the Spring Festival. I always have great fun.

Q2: Who uses the Internet the most in your family?
A2: My mum uses the Internet the most in my family. She uses it for work and daily life. She also likes shopping on Taobao and Jingdong.

Q3: What kind of TV programmes do you like to watch?
A3: I like Marvel movies, and Spider-Man is my favourite hero. He is cool and very kind.
